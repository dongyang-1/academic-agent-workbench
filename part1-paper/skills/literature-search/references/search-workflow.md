# Literature Search Workflow

## Intake questions

- What is the exact research question?
- Is the goal classic literature, frontier literature, review papers, empirical identification examples, or all of these?
- What fields are in scope: labor, development, public, IO, macro, finance, urban, environmental, health, education?
- What time window matters?
- Are there seed papers, authors, datasets, policies, regions, or JEL codes?

## Query construction

Use concept blocks and combine terms with Boolean logic.

```text
(concept A synonyms) AND (concept B synonyms) AND (context or method terms)
```

For economics topics, consider adding:

- JEL codes.
- Common policy names.
- Dataset names.
- Identification terms such as difference-in-differences, event study, IV, RDD, synthetic control, structural estimation.
- Working paper series terms such as NBER, CEPR, IZA, World Bank, IMF.

## Search sequence

1. Start with a broad discovery source to collect vocabulary and seed papers.
2. Search economics-specific databases and working paper repositories.
3. Search top journals and field journals when precision matters.
4. Use backward citation chasing for classic foundations.
5. Use forward citation chasing for newer related work.
6. Record queries and retrieval dates.

## Deduplication

Treat records as likely duplicates when they share:

- DOI.
- Exact or near-exact title.
- Same authors and year.
- Same working paper number.
- Working paper and published version relationship.

## Output table columns

Recommended minimum columns:

- Title.
- Authors.
- Year.
- Venue or series.
- DOI or URL.
- Retrieval source.
- Query used.
- Relevance note.
- Verification status.
