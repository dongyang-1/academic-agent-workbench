# Economics Literature Source Map

## Broad discovery

| Source | Use | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Scholar | Broad discovery, citation chasing, seed expansion. | Metadata can be noisy and hard to reproduce. |
| Semantic Scholar | Metadata, abstracts, citation graph, related papers. | Economics coverage varies by subfield. |
| OpenAlex | Open metadata and citation graph. | Requires metadata validation for key records. |
| Crossref | Broad DOI and publisher-deposited metadata. | Abstracts and citations are incomplete. |
| DataCite | Repository, dataset, report and DOI metadata. | Traditional journal coverage is secondary. |

## Economics-specific sources

| Source | Use | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IDEAS/RePEc | Economics working papers, author pages, series metadata. | Duplicate versions are common. |
| NBER | Frontier applied economics working papers. | Access to full papers may be limited. |
| CEPR | European and international working papers. | Some records may require access. |
| IZA | Labor economics and related applied topics. | Working paper versions may later publish elsewhere. |
| EconLit | Curated economics indexing. | Often requires institutional access. |
| EconBiz | Economics and business discovery with a public REST API. | Some records are reports or working papers rather than journal articles. |
| World Bank Documents & Reports | Development economics, regulation, institutions and policy research. | Keep publication type visible and do not treat every report as a peer-reviewed article. |

## Publisher and journal sources

| Source | Use | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AEA journals | Top general and applied economics journals. | Search interface may miss working paper versions. |
| Econometrica, QJE, JPE, ReStud, REStat | Top journal verification. | Use for final metadata validation. |
| Field journals | Subfield coverage and applied debates. | Select journals based on research field. |

## Preprint and adjacent sources

| Source | Use | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSRN | Finance, law and economics, early drafts. | Quality and version status vary. |
| arXiv | Econometrics, computation, ML-adjacent economics. | Less central for many economics topics. |
| World Bank, IMF, OECD | Policy reports and working papers. | Distinguish academic papers from institutional reports. |

## University research centres

| Source | Use | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicago BFI | Frontier economics working papers. | Many items also appear in SSRN or RePEc; deduplicate versions. |
| Stanford SIEPR | Policy-relevant economics working papers. | Official website is retained as a manual verification source. |
| LSE CEP | Labour, productivity, trade, urban and applied economics papers. | Official website is retained as a manual verification source. |
