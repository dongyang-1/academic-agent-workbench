# Literature Search Rubric

| Dimension | Good |
| --- | --- |
| Coverage | Captures classic papers, frontier papers, and field-specific sources. |
| Precision | Search terms match the research question and avoid excessive irrelevant records. |
| Traceability | Every candidate has retrieval source, query, and verification status. |
| Deduplication | Working paper and published versions are linked or merged correctly. |
| Usefulness | Output helps a researcher decide what to read next. |
